# The Plumber's Inbox - a 120-word cold email

An entry for make.fun's [The Plumber's Inbox: A 120-Word Cold Email](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ravendell-the-plumber-s-inbox-a-120-word-cold-email-9ab3e51c) bounty.

`main.py` writes one cold email to `/output/entry.txt`: a bookkeeping
pitch to a busy plumber, subject line included, 114 words in all. It
opens on the evenings paperwork eats, says exactly what the service
does each week and month, prices it against one callout fee, and ends
with one small ask - a free cleanup of last month. Before writing, it
checks the text against the gate (1 to 800 characters, not only white
space) and the brief's word count.
